# Gacha

## Overview

Gacha is a **mini-game loot-box system** inside MyShop and on the official website: rare items are "buried" in themed **Gacha Towns**, and players dig them up with special drills using **Gacha Coins**. Each town holds a collectible set of **9 rares**; completing the set awards a **4G Card** redeemable for exclusive equipment.

## How to play

1. Acquire **Gacha Coins** (MyShop: 1,000 points ≈ $1 each; packs of 5 / 11 / 23 coins; also event rewards and recharge promotions).
2. Open Gacha via the MyShop window (**Y** → Gacha "Click!") or the website (choose your World — Jewelia or Fantasia — when playing there).
3. Select a **Gacha Town**.
4. Choose your drill:
   - **Basic Drill** — 1 coin per dig (2 in Special Towns); can dig up anything (potions, accessories, PPADs...) — only **Treasure Chests** contain rares.
   - **Flicker Drill** — 2 coins per dig (3 in Special Towns); **always yields a rare item**.
5. Click a spot on the town map to drill, then click the Treasure Chest you unearthed to open it. Special Towns occasionally require more than 3 coins for a Flicker Drill.
6. Wins are delivered to your **MyShop inventory** (equipment) or MyCamp inventory's NEW tab (housing items).

## Towns and 4G Cards

Collect all 9 rares in a town → receive that town's **4G Card**, exchanged with **Rosemary in Megalopolis Square**:

| 4G Card | Gacha Town | Exchange prize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4G Card 1 | Gacha Town 1 — Megalopolis | Solar Sword (also Nocturnal Sword Lv 180) |
| 4G Card 2 | Gacha Town 2 — Oops Wharf | Solar Gun / cane (Nocturnal Staff Lv 180) |
| 4G Card 3 | Gacha Town 3 — Rose Garden | Solar Helmet / hat (Nocturnal Hat Lv 180) |
| 4G Card 4 | Gacha Town 4 — Azteca | Solar Shield (Nocturnal Shield Lv 180) |
| 4G Card 5 | Special Town — Tapasco | Solar Cape (Nocturnal Cape Lv 180; Solar Vest innerwear) |
| 4G Card Jr. | Beginner Town — Blooming Cora | Solar Jr. series (Lv 50 sword/gun/helmet/shield/cape) |

**Rotating sets:** each town's 9 rares refresh on a months-long rotation (ggFTW town pages) — Town 1 Megalopolis cycled Vigor → Time Keeper's → Chronos → Swashbuckler's → Skull → Card Master Taniel → Eltron → Panther → Sassy Vinosh → Rachel's → Leviathan (2012) sets; Town 2 Oops Wharf ran Pessimistic Pachi, Diploma, Trainer's, Rosie Pastel, Card Girl's, Portina's, Divine Spirit, Hunter Yuna, and Secretary Marion (2012). A set typically bundles a hat, weapon, shield, accessory/inner, pet, doll, and furniture with a set bonus; a dedicated **MyCamp Gacha** rotation (2012) carried dolls, chairs, racks, tables, and full decorations. Collecting the current 9 rares in time yields that town's 4G Card.

Normal filler items also appear in towns: Green Elixirs, Repair Powders, MyShop Pink/Blue potions, MyShop drills, etc.

## History

- Introduced in the **May 9, 2007** update: every existing account received 2 Gacha Coins; new accounts receive 1.
- New Gacha Towns shipped with major content updates (e.g. the April/May 2008 "Chaos Tower" patch also added new Gacha Towns).
- Gacha Coins were awarded as universal Poppuri event stage-4 rewards and recharge promotions.
