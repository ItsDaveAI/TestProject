# MyShop (Cash Shop)

## Overview

MyShop is Trickster Online's **cash shop**, the monetization of an otherwise free-to-play game: players buy **MyShop points** with real money (charged through the official website or the in-game recharge page) and spend them on equipment, pets, drills, consumables, cosmetics, and housing items. Open the shop in-game by pressing **Y**.

MyShop equipment is generally stronger than same-level free equipment and is often **non-tradable** (workarounds exist via [Item Fusion](11-item-fusion.md) skins and Trade Mail). Timed MyShop items expire and can be extended with **Recharge Coupons** via **Pia's Recharge Service** (Paradise/Megalopolis banks; 30 coupons = 2,700 pts).

## What MyShop sells (representative, ggFTW-era pricing)

| Category | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment | Level-tiered gear, often with randomized stats ("box equipment"), timed or permanent variants |
| Pets | Driller Boy / Driller Girl (2,900 pts, 15 days), Super Driller Boy (3,900 pts, 30 days) — see [Pets](12-pets.md) |
| Drills | Premium drills (Treble Drill bundled with Super Driller Boy; Flicker-class drilling) |
| Fusion/compound catalysts | **Artisan's Flame** (1,600 pts — [Item Fusion](11-item-fusion.md)), **Erin's Secret Book** (1,800 pts — Pet Fusion), Compound Elixirs, Nate's Bottles, Magic Stones, Capsules |
| Scrolls | Stat scrolls (e.g. DA Scroll: +11 DA for 240 s), Secret Scrolls (AP/MA/LK/HV/DA, personal and party versions), Warp Defense, Resolute, Illumination, Rejuvenation — also obtainable via [Recycling](24-recycling.md) |
| Cosmetics | Hair Dye Tickets (e.g. 2,700 pts per character/job), fashion sets, Disguise Kits |
| Utility | **Store-More Permit** (4,500 pts — permanently +8 warehouse slots and stored weight), Portable Port teleports (PPAD reaches gates/towns island-wide) |
| Gacha | [Gacha Coins](23-gacha.md) (1,000 pts each) |
| MyCamp | Furniture, wallpapers, exterior skins — see [MyCamp](21-mycamp.md) |

## Accessory boosters (Korean community meta)

MyShop accessories include **move-speed boosts (이속부/신속부)** and **weight boosts (무게부)**. Korean shopping advice (arca.live): the Lv 70 unlimited move-speed accessory carries the best weight among level-capped versions; Lv 150+ versions reach +90% and the Lv 270 version +100% — so either buy the cheap Lv 70 one or save directly for the 270. Weight-boost accessories are generally skipped because they occupy an accessory slot (invest the points in WT instead). Unlimited-duration mask/head fashion items are prized precisely because the bank lets you share them across characters.

Not all speed gear is cash: the **Brand accessories** exchanged from Harkon Protector drops and the free **Broken Speed Charm** every character receives are unlimited-duration alternatives — see [Harkon Protector](15-harkon-protector.md).

## Other behaviors

- **Gifting:** MyShop supports sending items as gifts (a listed in-game function; also used for couple/Love-Point building).
- **Mileage:** every 5,000 points spent earns a **Bonus Egg** (2 during Double Egg events) for the Egg Shop — see [Egg Shop & Pet Breeding](36-egg-shop-and-pet-breeding.md).
- **MyShop inventory:** press Y → the MyShop inventory is also where **Gacha wins**, level-up gift boxes, and milestone letters (e.g. the 3rd Job Guide at Lv 120, Eclipse's Message at Lv 180) are delivered.
- **Recharge promotions:** e.g. Feb 27–28, 2009 — one free Gacha Coin per 5,000 points recharged through the Trickster site/in-game page.
- **Revival servers:** LifeTO adds a **Galder→MyShop converter** (1,000 galders per point) in its control panel — a server-specific economy feature; xTrickster makes all MyShop items in-game-obtainable and non-expiring.
