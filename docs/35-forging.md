# Forging (Equipment Merging)

## Overview

With the Tower of Chaos came the **Forging System** — a Megalo Company service for improving equipment won in the tower (and from Random Boxes) by **merging two pieces of the same equipment into one** with, hopefully, better stats. This is the same system the Korean press called **혼돈 장비 융합 (Chaos equipment fusion)**, hosted by **Professor Komby (박사콤비)** on the tower's safe floors — see [Chaos Tower](28-chaos-tower.md) and [Korean version systems](33-korean-version-systems.md).

Two NPCs run it:

| NPC | Location | Accepts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Professor Komby** | Tower of Chaos safe (break) floors | Chaos, Requiem, and Altiverse equipment |
| **Lena** | Megalopolis Shop | Random Box equipment with the special forging icon |

## Chaos & Requiem forging

Requires **two pieces of the same equipment**. Place them in the first and second slots:

- If the **first-slot** item already has the best stats (or slots), its stats are **kept**.
- If the first-slot item is worse, the forged result **rolls randomly within the range between the two items' stats** — e.g. merging a Requiem Ruby Hat with 21 MA / 110 MD / 11 DA / 52 DP (3 slots) against one with 1 MA / 476 MD / 37 DP (2 slots) can land anywhere between their values per stat.

The order you slot the two copies therefore matters: put your better piece in first whenever it also has the slots you want.

## Altiverse forging

The safer variant: the **second-slot item's better stats/slots transfer directly into the first-slot item** (e.g. the second hat's higher AC is simply moved into the first). If the first item is already best, it keeps everything. No random rolling.

## Random Forging (Lena)

Lena forges only **special-icon Random Box equipment**. It works like Chaos/Requiem forging but is the risky version: there is a **small chance she destroys both equipments** in the attempt.
