# Shadow World & Mind's Eye

## Overview

The Shadow World is a hidden layer of Caballa Island visible only while the **Mind's Eye** skill is active — an Enlightenment-era system gated behind a hatched [Guardian](13-guardians.md). It unlocks daily Shadow quests, Shadow monsters and their equipment drops, and the entrance to [Harkon Protector](15-harkon-protector.md).

## Unlocking Mind's Eye

- Requirements: **level 180+**, a **hatched Guardian**, and **Eclipse's Message** (delivered automatically to your MyShop inventory when you reach 180).
- Quest: **Eclipse - Another View for the World** (Eclipse in the Garden of Skill Master): bring the **P. Anemone Card** (dropped by Popeyed Anemone Lv 105) and the **Dimensional Key** (from Eclipse - Aid from Eclipse in Eclipse's Dimension). Reward: the **Mind's Eye** skill.
- Mind's Eye is a **Timer skill** (MP 360, duration 80 s, cooldown 5 s) usable only while your Guardian is released, letting you see the Shadow World through the Guardian's power.

## The Shadow World

- **Shadow NPCs and monsters are invisible without Mind's Eye.** Eclipse herself only appears when the path to the Shadow World opens — in-game announcements fire 10 minutes and 2 minutes before each opening.
- **Eclipse appears in 8 fixed daily windows across the regions** (also 8 regional Shadow Sanctuaries — see [Harkon Protector](15-harkon-protector.md) for the schedule table) and offers teleports to that region's Shadow Sanctuary.
- Eclipse also runs **The Story of the Gods 1–12**, a lore quest series spanning the towns (Blooming Cora → Paradise → Megalopolis → Azteca → Oops Wharf → Aquarius → Ceremonia → Carbigal → Laplanoel → Gate of Techichi → Gate of Tapasco → Platonia), level ~210 era content.
- Shadow monsters also infest regular low-level dungeons at endgame levels — e.g. Poppuri Dungeon's Slide Cave hosts Shadow Kokebi (Lv 384), Shadow Simbatta (386), Shadow Gentle Sting (650), and Shadow Lucia (657).

## Daily Shadow quests

Available in **all regions** once Mind's Eye is active:

- **Shaman Girl Jia** — Shadow **Monster** quests, level-scaled: e.g. Lv 180–183: kill 3× Shadow Torobbie (Lv 324) in 15 min; Lv 184–187: 3× Shadow Bad Fury (Lv 327) in 10 min; rewards potions plus ~780k–820k base EXP / ~172k TM EXP per cycle.
- **Stella** — Shadow **Normal** quests (e.g. Shadow Star series in Megalopolis).

## Rewards and farming

- Shadow monsters drop **shadow/gloom equipment** (on LifeTO, gloom equipment farming at Path to Caballa Relics with Mind's Eye is a primary galder income), plus HP/MP potions and galders.
- Shadow daily quests are repeatable daily income and a source of **Star Tears EV** (up to 12/day on CoraTO) used to extend [Star Gazing](16-star-gazing.md) fortunes.
