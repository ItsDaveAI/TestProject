# Star Gazing (Fortune Reading)

## Overview

Star Gazing is a **fortune-buff system**: the NPC **Star Gazer Stella** reads the stars to give your character **temporary boosts to stats (AP, AC, DX, MA, MD, DA, LK, DP, HV), drop rate, refine rate, and/or base EXP**. Results depend on **Fortune Cards, your level, and real luck**.

## Unlocking

Complete Stella's short quest chain at **Desert Beach Field 2 - Sand Palace** (Lv 15+):

1. Talk to **Star Gazer Stella (2) - The Star Gazer** (choose polite dialogue options — insulting her ends the conversation).
2. She needs a business license: talk to **Don Giuvanni** (top-right of the same map), who demands soap — 1 per cycle, 3 cycles total (Soap is drilled in Desert Beach Field 3 - Statue of Anubis; cycles 1–2 reward 500 Galder Coupons, cycle 3 the Don Giuvanni Card).
3. Return to Stella: rewards the **Stars and Fortunes Book** (also sold by Item Girl for 100 g — it explains the system), a **Star Card Pack No. 5**, and unlocks Star Gazing.

Until this quest is done, Stella will not read your fortune at **any** of her business locations (her tent in Blooming Cora, Sand Palace, Megalopolis Square — where she also runs a Lv 35 daily quest, service cost from 1,000 galders).

## Fortune Cards

- Obtained by opening **Star Card Packs** — common monster drops, plus quests and events. Pack series: **No.1–No.7** plus themed packs (New Year, Merry Christmas, Year of the Hare, 6th Anniversary).
- Stored in the **Fortune** tab of the Card inventory; cards register like skill cards.
- Each card carries one of **13 effect types** — the nine combat stats plus **Drop rate, EXP, Refine, and Joker** — at a **power number 1–6** (6 strongest).
- Card numbers encode stat + power: **AP = No.2–9** (Torobbie's power), **AC = No.12–19**, **DX = No.22–29** (Blue Penguin), **MA = No.32–39** (Clione), **MD = 42–49**, **DA = 52–59**, **LK = 62–67**, **HV = 85–89**, **Refine = 91–95**, **Drop = 100–101+**, **EXP = 103–107** — e.g. Star Card No.2 AP, No.12 AC, No.22 DX, No.32 MA.

## The reading

1. Stella lays out **10 random cards**. Once revealed, you cannot back out without paying.
2. **Swap cards:** replace Stella's cards with your own (limited swaps per day; swapped cards are consumed even if pointers miss them; swapped cards fill the reading circle right-to-left).
3. **Spin:** click Proceed and spin the wheel — **three pointers** land on cards; you gain the stats of the cards they hit.
4. **Stacking:** pointers landing on the same card or same stat type give a bigger boost.
5. **Re-roll:** pay double for a new spin if you dislike the outcome.

Boosts last **3 hours** (CoraTO-era rule), can be extended with **Star Tears** (MyShop; or earned from Shadow dailies — up to 12/day), and getting a new reading early **overwrites** (loses) current boosts.

## Strategy tips (community)

- Cluster swapped cards together (start or end of the arc) rather than spreading them.
- Place your cards near matching cards Stella holds to raise the odds of stacking a desired stat.
