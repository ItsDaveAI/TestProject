# I Want to Be a Summoner (Board-RPG Successor, 2015)

## Overview

**소환사가 되고싶어 ("I Want to Be a Summoner", for Kakao)** is the forgotten middle successor — a **mobile board RPG** by **Entrivbr Soft itself** (the original studio, not NCSOFT), first shown at **G-STAR 2014** and launched **April 21, 2015**, reusing Trickster's dot sprites, sound, and systems wholesale: **800+ characters** drawn from the MMO's cast, the **growth-compound** terminology carried over by name, **Harkon and galders** recycled as its currencies. Korean service ended **January 9, 2017** (the Japanese server ran on to **April 25, 2019**). An earlier mobile Trickster (via SK Networks Internet) had flopped before it — fans entered this one fearing "corpse-selling," but the VIP test won goodwill, and after Trickster M's reception, the community **re-evaluated it as "the last real Trickster game"** — the only successor made by the original developers.

## Systems

### Board-RPG movement (모험 / Adventure)
Stages are laid out as **board-game tracks**: each move offers three number cards (the middle one face-up, the outer two hidden) — pick one to advance that many spaces, landing on monster battles, gold, or HP recovery. Stage **challenge objectives** demand landing on specific spaces, so card-picking is the skill. Turn-based combat supports two auto-battle modes (with or without skills; the without-mode saves MP for bosses), and MP-filled units cast by a screen-swipe.

### Attributes
A different wheel from the MMO's five elements: a **Water / Fire / Wood triangle**, plus **Light and Dark in mutual opposition**. Starter characters: **Ignis** (Fire), **Seria** (Water), **Nanna** (Wood), joined by **De Ankete** (Dark) and **Juiss** (Light) — basic cards evolve to 6★.

### Affection system (호감도)
NPCs carry **affection meters** — one free daily conversation plus **gift boxes** from content raise them, with rewards per affection tier. Raising **Maid Anje's** affection summoned her card and paid **30 Harkon**; the launch event handed out the mascot **Kuu** card for seven daily logins.

### Collection & shops
- **Summon Shop / Honey Shop** — the gacha layer: friend-point pulls for basic cards, and paid "new summon" sets (Girls I Want, Weapon Master, Magic Club, Card Master, Divine Beast Tamer, Fantasy Party, Toy Children, Artist Collection, Occult Parade, Möbius Incident, Genius Record, Interracial Academy, Pajama Party, Four Gentlemen series), plus limited and event summons — 800+ cards total.
- **Secret shop, party score, battle zone, and the dimensional barrier (차원의 결계)** — the high-difficulty stage line whose fifteen story chapters (Serapis's Justice, Kaihl's Wrath, Velik's Seal…Persona's Awakening, the Grand Duke's Successor) carried the endgame.
- **Treasure excavation (보물 발굴)** and the **growth-compound system** — drilling and compounding by name, straight from the MMO.

### The Japanese server
Outlived the Korean one (to 2019) and hosted the collaborations: **Pangya**, **HACKA DOLL**, **SHOW BY ROCK!!**, and Square Enix's **Sangokushi Ranbu** — plus premium summons that brought the **original cast back as cards**: "Librarian **Sheep**" and "Shaman **Dragon**" among them.

## Documented problems (era critique)
Type system and support-card stacking, steep SP consumption, and legendary-dungeon difficulty — the usual live-service complaints, none IP-destroying.
