# Progression Guide — How the Systems Fit Together

## Overview

A level-ordered walk through the whole game — where each system unlocks and what it feeds. Use it as the reading order companion to the per-system files. (Level gates reflect the official international service; the Korean endgame extension continues past them.)

**The official region ladder (client data):** the client's own world-map guide (`WorldMap_RegionInfo`) pins every region to its intended level band, with official flavor text: **Cora Beach Lv 1–15** (the beginner village built by Megalo Company) → **Desert Beach Lv 15–30** (the "miracle of the desert," Pyramid Dungeon beyond) → **Caballa Relics Lv 50–60** (the Alteo-ruin city, Tombeth below) → **Mermaid Palace Lv 70–90** → **Mirage Island Lv 80–100** (where "legendary figures of Caballa Island" are met in person — the episode-quest region) → **Ghost Blue Lv 95–110** (the ship-graveyard "devil's zone," Nora Sewers via Don Juvanni in Aquarius) → **Rose Garden Lv 115–130** (maze garden, Vamp Castle) → **Black Swamp Lv 135–150** (lily-pad travel) → **Snow Hill Lv 160–190** (permanent-Christmas town, Blue Ice Dungeon) → **Techichi Volcano Lv 210–240** (the ash-fossilized village, Black Ash) → **Tapasco Volcano Lv 230–270** (the pepper-gas volcano, Theme Spa).

## The first day (Lv 1–30)

1. **Create your character** ([types](01-character-types-and-creation.md)) — pick the Build Graph; you keep the free **Broken Speed Charm** (+30% speed, forever).
2. **Episode 0 tutorial** at Blooming Cora — Bunny Maid's registration, Driller Marky's first **drill** ([05](05-drilling.md)), Marx's refining quiz (5 Bronze Gemstones), Stella's star-quest unlock on Desert Beach Field 2 (Lv 15).

   The client's **Notice (노티스) system** walks newcomers through exactly this: an exclamation-mark notification companion (disableable via the Option window, `O`) that announces the Lv 15 gift box mailing, points each type to its **skill-card vendor in Paradise** (Power → **Luis**, Magic → **Sephyra**, Sense → **Kokuma**, Charm → **Tifaret**; the same cards at Megalopolis's Skill Master Garden), flags the **key-quest start level** (Sandman Kim in Paradise), and the **Crazy Drilling quest level** (Driller Markut). It also delivers two hard warnings: **at Lv 21 EXP protection and same-spot revival end**, and **at Lv 50 "chick graduation" — you may become someone's Angel** ([44](44-mentor-and-support-systems.md)).
3. **Bank the staples**: 15 Coins, 5× 50-galder and 2× 500-galder coupons dug everywhere; sell Lv 30 mail ears/tails for starter galders.
4. **Desert Beach / Paradise** (Lv 20–39 band): skill NPCs (buy skill cards, [03](03-tm-leveling-and-skills.md)), the **Crazy Drilling** questline in Paradise, Love Hunter Robin's **TM quests** (your TM must not fall behind), the Pyramid, and the **Lv ≤30 Master Mong** bout (3 Addax Horns → Monkey T).
5. **Megalopolis** (Lv 40–59 band): the capital's services — bank, guild office (join for the ranking EXP bonus), Garden of Skill Master, Monster Guild. Scenario 1 Chapter 1 (Nefertiti's Necklace, Lv 45) ends in your first unique-tier accessory.

## The midgame (Lv 30–130)

- **Fiesta Zones from Lv 21** ([29](29-fiesta-zone.md)) — the TM engine; run your bracket twice a day.
- **Poppuri Dungeon** — the four Trials, the Mysterious Space, and (at **Lv 65, 2nd job**) the **Card Identification** chain ([07](07-card-identification.md)).
- **2nd job at Lv 60 / TM 50** ([04](04-job-advancement.md)): Kaboom's Growth Badge + 3× 100k checks → your class Sign, new sprite, **capes**. Hybrid skill trees cost +50 TM.
- **Scenario 1 Ch. 2–3** (Lv 50–52): Oops Wharf pieces → the Weird Box's "Vendetta" → the Harkon Seekers.
- **Boss circuit**: Tombeth (Lv 1–150 entry, 500-galder coupon + 60 m drill, 20-min cooldown), then Tutankhamen (≤90, the Golden Sword), Captain Skull, and the trial bosses as you level ([27](27-bosses.md)).
- **Chaos Tower** from 1st job ([28](28-chaos-tower.md)) — cyclical floors, rest-floor teleports, the Forging economy.
- **Equipment lifecycle** ([08](08-refinement.md)/[09](09-maturing-compound.md)/[10](10-tempering.md)): refine (break = Repair Powder) → compound (LK, elixirs) → temper at Lv 20+ (Chaos's Feathers from PQs/Treasure Hunts/Card ID).
- **Episode 2 (Lv 80+)** at Mirage Island; **3rd-job guide book** arrives at Lv 120.

## The endgame gate (Lv 130–180)

1. **3rd job at Lv 130 / TM 120** — the Door of Judgment (pure left / hybrid right; Dark Lord red), the Harkon via Marx's Snow Hill mine quest, the 16 Tribulation Rooms, guardian stones from your Job Master.
2. **Episode 3 (Lv 130+)** — Hero's Testament → the **Dev Room Card Key**.
3. **Soul Seed at Lv 135** — keep the Questionable Letter; the Trickster Cathedral chain matures your **Poseidon's Seed** ([13](13-guardians.md)).
4. **Guild life** — GvG colosseum battles ([18](18-guild-system.md)), weekly ranking EXP, Boss Battle mode against Tutankhamen G→Spicy.
5. **Awakening at Lv 180**: 16 Secret Cards (burn 1,000+ via Card ID *before* base empathy for the Legendary route) → Secret Space Map → the doppelganger fight (strip your gear) → **Mind's Eye**.

## The enlightenment endgame (Lv 180–400)

- **Shadow World** dailies via Eclipse ([14](14-shadow-world.md)) — Jia's monster hunts, Stella's quests, shadow/gloom gear.
- **Harkon Protector** six runs a day ([15](15-harkon-protector.md)) — drops converted into the Mega/Giga/Tera **Brand** speed charms at Happy Stollon.
- **Star Gazing** ([16](16-star-gazing.md)) with Refine/Drop/EXP fortune cards; **Wedding** ([20](20-wedding-system.md)) for the partner teleport; **Fiesta** brackets to 400.
- **Korean-service extension** ([33](33-korean-version-systems.md)): True Soul Space past the 72F boss, the **Melting** system, **Tartarus** (Koius → True Chronos) and the **Soul Weapon** ladder, the Theme Spa, Pola (Lv-graph builds, DA-scaling).
