# Soul Seed & Soul Guardians

## Overview

The **Guardian system** (Enlightenment content, Season 2 era) gives every character a permanent **familiar that follows you and automatically assists in PvE combat using skills** (explicitly not PvP). Guardians are grown, not bought: the process runs Card Identification → Soul Seed → Guardian creation → Empathy building. You can begin as early as **level 65** (the NA-era chain), with the full awakening completing at level 180.

## The process

1. **Card Identification** (see [07-card-identification.md](07-card-identification.md)) — done *before* planting the seed; collect items and cards beforehand to speed everything up.
2. **Soul Seed quests** — the **Poseidon Seed** must be obtained and **matured** through the Soul Seed quest chain (guide total: 24,321,198 base EXP / 5,374,174 TM EXP; items include 500 Galder Coupons, Candied Apples, Flower of Revivals, Spicy/Fried Eggs, Great potions, the Don Danihen Card, Eclipse's Message, Guardian Guide, Summon Guardian).
3. **Guardian creation** — the matured seed hatches into your Guardian.
4. **Empathy building** — empathy is a **hidden point system** that builds the guardian's stat graph (visually similar to your MyView growth graph) through feeding/interaction. You cannot see empathy itself, only the resulting stat levels.

## Guardian facts

- **Grades:** normal, superior, mighty, and **legendary** guardians (Mew's community guide: a first legendary took ~10 days with minimal preparation, 3–5 days with good preparation; a skilled player finished one in a day).
- **Skills:** a guardian can use any skill flagged with a **green marker** (guardian-usable); builds are planned around which skills you feed.
- Guardians fight **PvE only**.
- **Mind's Eye** — the follow-on Enlightenment skill that lets you see the Shadow World — requires a hatched Guardian (see [Shadow World](14-shadow-world.md)).
- Higher-level Enlightenment quests (Janus's Secret Hideaway, Lv 180+ 3rd job with matured Poseidon Seed) continue from here: Living Flame collection from Red Flame Spirits, etc.

## Guardian forms and graph mechanics

- **Five guardian forms**, each tied to a graph shape: **Sephirens** (Guardian of Power, skill "Power Source" — claws and gale-wings), **Digrimm**, **Ifron**, **Krusef**, and **Spinel**. If multiple graphs tie, the form is decided by which behaviors promoted those types most.
- Each form exists in grades: **Legendary / Mighty / Superior / Accomplished**.
- Community-measured graph rules (LifeTO guide): **1,000 aura points = 1 graph point** (A/M/S/C graph); the process stops at a **14-point total** (the Mighty cap) or when any single graph reaches **6 points**; 0-point graphs automatically become 1 after hatching; **graph points equal the number of guardian skill slots**; and reaching **18 total points (base + secret empathy) guarantees a Legendary** — the secret-empathy route burns 1,000+ cards via Card Identification *after* equipping the Soul Seed but *before* starting base empathy.
- **The rune carry-over quirk** (Mew's guide): passives gained through a **Louis's rune** (Faint, Guard Break) keep boosting skills like Shockwave even after the rune is swapped for **Cochma** (Shield Breaker + Lucky Fist) — actives don't stick, passives do. This is the backbone of the community's "cookie-cutter" Lucky Fist guardian build.
- **The Accomplished trap:** if your secret-empathy +2 lands *on* the type you wanted, that guardian becomes unobtainable — the +2 must miss the target graph, which is what separates an Accomplished outcome from a Mighty/Legendary one.
- LifeTO ships a Soul Seed Viewer quality-of-life tool for planning graphs.

## The Korean awakening chain (각성)

Korean guides sequence the whole Enlightenment line by level: **1st advancement (Lv 60) → Card Identification (Lv 65) → 2nd advancement (Lv 130) → Soul Seed (Lv 135) → Awakening (Lv 180)**.

- **Soul Seed (Lv 135):** the **Questionable Letter (의문의 편지)** arrives by mail at 135 — do not discard it (the chain cannot even start without it). Take it to the **Trickster Cathedral** (Mirage Island Field 7); the **Shining Egg** sends you to the elemental **Nephtri**, who routes you to **Eclipse** for the item-gathering steps. Nephtri then awards the **Poseidon's Seed**, and a second conversation grants the **Dimensional Card Pack** (which can drop Secret Cards — needed for awakening).
- **Awakening (Lv 180):** with Card Identification and Soul Seed completed, combine **16 Secret Cards** into the **Secret Space Map**, then run a treasure-map-style dig (within 1 hour of using the map) to reach the hidden space. The questline includes collecting items (soul essences from Vamp Castle monsters via Lavida; life-source seeds from Swamp Dungeon monsters via the Frog Prince; the god's elixir from the Rose Garden prophetesses — timed hunts of Changed Dancer Michel / Changed Captain Norman), and famously **fighting your own doppelganger** — it copies your equipment, so enter that fight with your gear unequipped. Delivering the final items to Eclipse in the Garden of Skill Master grants the **Mind's Eye** skill, and an announcement broadcast celebrates the awakening (guild members congratulate you).
- **Empathy mechanics (교감):** feeding and drilling build the hidden graph — roughly **12,500 Pink Potion A** for Power 5 / Charm 3; **Blue Potion A** at ~2,500 per Magic point and ~5,000 per Sense point; and deliberate **empty drilling** (50 empty digs = 1 flash of light, 20 flashes = 1 graph point). Target builds such as Power/Magic/Sense/Charm **5/3/3/3** aim at a legendary guardian (e.g. **Sephirens** or **Spinel**; magic builds want Sephirens). Feeding is done through the seed/guardian's secret communion interface while you keep drilling or eating — expect sore fingers.
