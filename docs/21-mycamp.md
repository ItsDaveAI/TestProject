# MyCamp (Player Housing)

## Overview

MyCamp is Trickster's **housing/decoration system**: every character can pitch a personalized tent that other players can enter and visit. Furnishings, wallpapers, and exterior "skins" are bought with MyShop points, with **free items unlocking as you level** through the game.

## Setting up

1. Right-click your own character and click the **tent icon** (top right of the menu).
2. Configure the camp:
   - **Name** — shown above the tent to everyone (choose carefully; you can later edit it to something longer from inside the camp via the pencil icon under the exterior preview).
   - **Status** — **Open** (anyone may enter) or **Private** (password-protected; password set in the revealed text box).
   - **Capacity** — 2 to 16 characters.
3. Other players enter by clicking the camp's floating name/text box (double-click the white textbox above it).

## Inside the camp

- Owner controls (in-camp menu): room details (title/status/max), member list, **Options** (same as the O-key options window), **Deco** (decoration mode), **Out** (leave; the camp stays pitched for a while), **Exit** (leave and close the camp). Visitors get member list, Options, and Exit.
- **Deco Mode vs Chatting Mode** — Deco Mode is the editing state for placing furniture; Chatting Mode is the social state.
- Only **one layout can be saved per character** (a client-code limitation).
- Thai-era documentation of the same system confirms the setup flow: Select Camp (when owning more than one exterior), Select Mode, Room Title, Status (open/private), and Max capacity.

## Decorating and the MyCamp shop

- Open the decoration screen by pressing **Shop** inside your camp, or via **MyShop (Y) → MyCamp button** (top right). The MyCamp inventory holds owned items; new purchases land under the **NEW** tab.
- You can **preview placements before buying** — nothing is charged until you commit the layout.
- Double-left-click an item in the shop to see its name and price; use the pink **Shopping** icon in the Article Manage menu to purchase.
- Item types: chairs, beds, wallpapers, exterior skins, decorations, and more — many themed around in-game NPCs.
- Free items unlock as you level (check back periodically — the unlocks surprise people).
- Decorating is expensive: MS-point costs add up quickly, as every wiki warns.
