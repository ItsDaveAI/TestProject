# PvP

## Overview

Trickster Online offers three PvP formats, all centered on the **Colosseum** in Event Garden - Ceremonia and the **Phantom Dungeon**: consensual duels, free-for-all battlefields, and an open-world skirmish dungeon with monsters. Player battles run on private fields for points and prizes; spectators can watch.

## Entering

- Talk to **Bunny Maid** in Event Garden - Ceremonia and give her **1 Pink Potion A** — 15 g at Blooming Cora's shop (30 g elsewhere), also dropped by Coral/Desert Beach monsters, drilled in low-level maps, and awarded by the Coral Beach daily quest — and she sends you to the **Colosseum Waiting Room** (her transfer quest lists level 1+; the arena itself requires level 2+).
- On LifeTO the colosseums are split into a **Lower Colosseum** (Lv 1–200, via Bunny Maid) and **Higher Colosseum** (Lv 201+, via Miranda Watty) — a revival-server arrangement of the same maps.

## Battle modes

### Free Match
- No attack restrictions — everyone can hit everyone.
- Maps: **Battlefield of Desert** and **Battlefield of Garden**, entered by talking to **Rosemary** (1,000 galders per entry).

### Duel Mode
- Consent-based: right-click a player to see their level/points and challenge; the fight begins only if they accept.
- Map: **Battlefield of Duel**, entered from the Colosseum Waiting Room by giving **Don Giuvanni 3× Compound Waste**.
- **Bunny Maid** waits inside this battlefield to give rewards to winners.

### Skirmish — Phantom Dungeon
- Entered free via **Shaman Girl Jia** at the Gate of Phantom School (in front of Phantom School 1-1).
- An open dungeon (~Lv 180 monsters) where **you choose your stance**:
  - **Peace Mode** — you cannot attack players and they cannot attack you (hunt monsters/quest in safety).
  - **Active Mode** — you may attack other players and be attacked.
- A scoreboard tracks your kills and defeats.
- Monster drops: potions, galders, **Phantom weapons** (e.g. Phantom Pugio) and Stones/Crystals/Metals 185.
- Hazards: **Ghost Books** self-destruct (they say "Guaah!" first); the Dark Ball monster teleports you to Megalopolis.

## Notes

- Community "PvP wars" between guilds/teams were commonly arranged on the free battlefields (documented in player videos from the official era).
- Card Battle (see [06-card-battle.md](06-card-battle.md)) is the non-combat PvP counterpart; GvG covers mass guild battles (see [18-guild-system.md](18-guild-system.md)).
