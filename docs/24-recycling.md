# Item Recycling

## Overview

Introduced with **Trickster Season 2 (announced January 5, 2011 by SG Interactive)**, the recycling system lets players exchange **unwanted MyShop items** for **Recycling Tickets**, which are then traded for useful consumables. It exists because players out-leveled purchased gear with no way to recover value.

## The service

- **Recyclek Mk1** NPC, located in the **Paradise Shop**, **Megalopolis Shop**, and **Laplanoel Shopping Mall** (Snow Hill) — mirrors of the same vendor.
- Hand in old MyShop items → receive **Recycling Tickets**.
- Exchange tickets for scrolls and consumables at no additional galder cost on the ggFTW-era exchange menu:

| Tickets | Item |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Basic Detection Scroll (item detection 80 m) |
| 2 | Super Detection Scroll (15 s), Party Detection Scroll (20 s party), Warp Defense Scroll (300 s), Scroll of the Resolute (faint defense), Scroll of Illumination (light +), Scroll of Rejuvenation (HP recovery 50% nearby) |
| 2 | Secret Scroll (AP / MA / LK / HV / DA) — +100% of player level stat boosts |
| 2 | Secret Scroll party variants — +60% of party members' level |
| 3 | Higher-tier exchanges on the same menu |

(The exact item-to-ticket mapping above follows the ggFTW exchange table; PandaTO's version additionally traded Disguise Kits for 3 tickets + 200,000 galders, and its shop was later removed from that server — server-specific variations.)

**The Korean-era VIP exchange shop (client data):** the late Korean client added a **VIP Shop** with a **100-item exchange catalogue** (`VIPShop` + three `VIPShopItem` page tables) pricing items in recycle-style points: a **Light-equipment box for 13 points**, classic skins (mini-hat, shield) for **3 points** each — the same old-item-for-points economy, formalized as a permanent shop rather than an NPC menu.

## Positioning

Recycling sits alongside the other value-recovery and appearance systems: [Item Fusion/Defusion](11-item-fusion.md) (turn looks into tradable Form items), and the MyShop [gifting](22-myshop.md) function. The official press framing: "exchange unwanted, older My Shop items for coupons which can then be exchanged for useful items."
