# Tempering

## Overview

Tempering is the third equipment-modification system (added in the Season 2 era), distinct from [Refinement](08-refinement.md) and [Maturing Compound](09-maturing-compound.md): it **re-rolls an equipment's base stats** randomly within a **70%–130%** band of their current values. It can be done an **unlimited number of times with no failure rate**. Service NPC: **Alan** (Paradise Shop, Megalopolis Shop, Carbigal Shop, Laplanoel Shopping Mall).

## Mechanics

- Example: an item with 100 AP re-tempers to between 70 and 130 AP; 200 DP becomes 140–260 DP.
- Tempering **re-rolls all base stats the equipment has at once** — you cannot pick a single stat.
- The result is **random, not based on LK**.
- Tempering does **not** affect compound slots (number or type) — slots and their compounded stats are untouched.

**Special case — randomized-stat equipment:** "box equipment" (generally MyShop), Ultimate, Chaos, and Master equipment with inherently randomized stats don't re-roll around current values; tempering re-randomizes within that item's possible min/max range.

## Requirements and cost

- Only equipment **Lv 20 and above** can be tempered; the feather must match the equipment's level bracket (unlike compounding, there is no "higher item on lower gear" allowance — the item must be within range).
- **Exclusions:** Unique Sets (e.g. the Pharaoh set) and Phantom equipment Lv 215/275 (PandaTO later allowed tempering boss uniques and Lv 185 Phantom gear — server-specific).
- Cost: **3× Chaos's Feather** per temper, plus a galder fee of **item value × 1.75** (0-value items: level × 100).
- On PandaTO the feather cost was reduced from 3 to 1 (server-specific change).

## Chaos's Feathers

Feather tiers ladder by 15 levels (feather level = the low end of its bracket):

| Feather | Equipment level |
| --- | --- |
| Chaos's Feather 20 | 20–34 |
| Chaos's Feather 35 | 35–49 |
| Chaos's Feather 50 | 50–64 |
| Chaos's Feather 65 | 65–79 |
| ... | ... (each +15) |
| Chaos's Feather 185 | 185+ |

**Sources:** Officer Tera's regional **Party Quests** (dungeons and mines), Keeper Julio's Storage Missions, region **Treasure Hunts**, and **Card Identification**. Gold Gems (from Level Up Gift Boxes on PandaTO) can increase the tempering range for certain stats.
